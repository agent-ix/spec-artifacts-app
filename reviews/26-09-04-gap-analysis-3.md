# SR-008: Gap analysis of Plan-001, the semantic module contract (issue #3)

## Summary

Post-implementation verification of `plan/Plan-001-semantic-module-contract/`
against the shipped tree, run at `f13fbd1` in the
`spec/3-semantic-module-contract` worktree. Read-only: no code, spec, plan or
config was changed by this review, and nothing was committed.

**The mechanical result is good.** All ten tasks are `status: done`, and every
subtask checkbox in every task file is checked. Every deliverable each task
names exists: `typespec/main.tsp` and `tspconfig.yaml`, `scripts/generate-schemas.mjs`
with a working `--check`, 39 emitted schemas plus `toolchain.json`, the manifest
at 0.2.0 with a nine-key `semantic` block and two reference-form `data_schema`
entries, `mappings.yaml` with its schema and its `build_mappings.py` generator,
three skeletons, eight negative fixtures, the vendored FR-035 schema, the
legacy-manifest fixture, the pre-change `spec/spec.md` baseline, and
`tests/support/{import_graph,reference_mapping}.py`. `make lint` is green,
`make schemas-check` is green on the committed tree (39 schemas, mappings and
legacy fixture all matching), `make test` is 43 passed / 2 xfailed, and
`quire validate` exits 0 over both `spec/**/*.md` and `plan/**/*.md` (9 advisory
EARS/quality warnings remain in FR-003 and FR-004; none is structural).

**Every trace tag binds, and the three known traps are all clear.** All 44
`@pytest.mark.trace` markers sit directly above a `def test_…` — none is wrapped
by `black` across lines (agent-ix/quire-rs#395), none sits on a module docstring
or a plain helper, and the only bare `TC-` ids outside a marker are two string
literals (`"TC-101"`) inside a dict in `tests/test_mappings.py`, which mint
nothing. `test_imports.py`'s module docstring says so explicitly. `quire coverage`
independently reports zero untracked symbols and zero non-binding tags.

**What the numbers count, precisely.** They are four different populations and
none of them is the others:

- `quire coverage --scope .` headline **77/81 rows backed (95%)**. The 81 are
  *minted targets*: 38 FR acceptance criteria (`FR-001-AC-1..4`, `FR-002-AC-1..11`,
  `FR-003-AC-1..8`, `FR-004-AC-1..8`, `FR-005-AC-1..7`), 4 NFR acceptance
  criteria, 3 StR validation criteria, and the 36 `TC-` rows of the Test Case
  Summary. The four unbacked are `FR-001-AC-2`, `FR-001-AC-3`, `FR-001-AC-4` and
  `StR-001-VC-1`. The 14 `CON` rows and the 5 `IT` acceptance criteria are **not
  in this population at all** — no integration document mints a row here — so a
  green `CON` row is invisible to the headline (FND-001, FND-003).
- The Test Matrix's own criterion population is **64 rows** — 47 AC + 14 CON +
  3 VC — of which 55 are `✅` and 9 are `🚧`. Different population, different
  number.
- The Test Case Summary is **36 rows**, one per `TC-NNN`, 33 `✅` and 3 `🚧`.
  All 36 bind.
- `quire`'s third number, the binding census, is **44/44/45 bound/tagged/candidates**:
  45 test symbols exist, 44 carry a tag, all 44 bind. The one untagged symbol is
  `test_pack_exposes_manifest_path` (FND-009).

**Code is traced to spec, with one shipped surface that is not.** Everything in
the module payload — the manifest, the 39 schemas, `mappings.yaml`, the three
skeletons — is owned by FR-002..FR-005, and every script under `scripts/` except
one is an FR-002 or FR-004 deliverable. The exception is the npm packaging path:
`scripts/stage-npm.mjs`, the `prepack`/`postpack` hooks and
`.github/workflows/release-npm.yml` ship real behaviour that no requirement
states and no test exercises — and the risk-complexity review predicted exactly
this defect class before implementation began (FND-007).

**The issue's fixture deliverable is honest, with one qualification.**
*Generated-language* fixtures are declared out of scope in `spec/spec.md` naming
their owning tickets (`agent-ix/filament-core-data#21`, `#22`, `#23`, `#11`) and
the promotion gate (`agent-ix/quoin#290`); nothing is faked in their place, and
that is the right call. *Compatibility* fixtures are real and load-bearing:
`tests/fixtures/manifest-legacy.yaml` is regenerated by
`scripts/build_legacy_manifest.py` and gated by `make schemas-check`, and
`tests/fixtures/baseline/spec.md` is the genuine pre-change document
(`## Purpose` / `## Module Summary` / `## Structure`), not a copy of the current
one. *Dynamic-module* fixtures are the qualification: they are synthesized in
`tests/test_imports.py` as the ticket's deliverable requires, but the
`manifest.yaml` files that test writes into `tmp_path` are never read back —
`find_cycles` takes an in-memory dict — so the on-disk half of the fixture is
inert and the docstrings that say otherwise overstate it (FND-005).

**The four ticket acceptance criteria.** (1) *Reference imported types rather
than duplicating fields* — **met**: `ImportedTypeRef {module, type}`,
`semantic.imports` pinned to `agent-ix/spec-artifacts-iso: 0.2.0`,
`imported_types` in `mappings.yaml`, TC-010 asserting no duplicated field and no
cross-module `$ref`, TC-024 asserting the cell parse. (2) *Every structured
section typed or explicitly prose-only* — **met**: TC-022 enumerates every level-2
heading of every skeleton against a `mappings.yaml` entry or a `prose_only` +
`reason` pair. (3) *Existing valid application specs remain readable* — **met for
the one sample that exists**: TC-018 validates and maps the committed pre-change
baseline; no corpus repository is in scope, so "existing specs" is measured
against a population of one, which `spec/spec.md` records rather than hides.
(4) *Cross-module cycles and missing imports fail clearly* — **met inside this
repository only**: four distinct diagnostics with deterministic cycle ordering,
all implemented in `tests/support/import_graph.py`, which is test support. The
module ships no diagnostic and no published engine performs the check (FND-010).

**The three safety-gate items.** *Advisory-only until ecosystem promotion* —
**met**: `legacy_forms: warning`, `compatibility_posture: additive`,
`lint_rules: []`, no corpus edited, and `spec/spec.md` records that the published
quoin carries no `semantic` reader at all, so the block is inert to every
installable consumer. *No UI implementation change bundled* — **met**: the module
declares `UiSurface` and `RenderingRequirement` as data and renders nothing;
there is no UI code in the tree. *Import changes require compatibility fixtures*
— **met**: the import set is new in 0.2.0 and `tests/fixtures/manifest-legacy.yaml`
(block and every `data_schema` removed) is proved to validate under the same
FR-035 schema and to register the same artifact types, by TC-011 and TC-034.

Fourteen findings: seven medium, seven low, no high. The optional semantic
review (step 4, intent↔test↔code) was **not** run — it was not requested — so
nothing below is a judgment about whether a test validates its requirement's
intent, only about whether the binding and the claim are real.

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL** — every task is done, every one of the 36 Test Case rows binds
to a real tagged symbol, and no finding is `high`. What keeps this from PASS is
that four of the matrix's own claims about itself do not survive being checked:
one `✅` criterion row has no trace tag anywhere in the suite (FND-001), the
Overview's "exactly one test symbol per `TC-NNN`" is contradicted by eight
shared ids (FND-002), the Overview's account of the unbacked set names six rows
where `quire` counts four different targets (FND-003), and the engine skipped
status classification entirely on the Functional Requirement Coverage table, so
its `status_lies: []` is vacuous for 52 of the 64 criterion rows (FND-004). None
of these is a hole in the tested behaviour; all four are the matrix asserting
more than it can back, which is the thing this gate exists to catch. FND-005 and
FND-006 are two task subtasks marked done whose stated mechanism is not present
in the tree, and FND-007 is one shipped surface with no owning requirement.

## Coverage

every task file checked; no blocked or in-progress task; no stale checkbox in

(FND-005, FND-006).

`untracked_symbols: []`, and a python binding census of **44/44/45**

`strict=True` expected failure or a manual-gate test naming its upstream issue:
`FR-003-AC-8`/`IT-002-AC-2` (agent-ix/quire-rs#394, with a negative control

about the `✅` markers there.

**Trace-tag traps.** All three checked and clear. No `black`-wrapped

form and mints nothing.

build plumbing rather than module behaviour and predates this plan. Nothing

masquerading as complete was found: the negative fixtures are routed by their
own `expect` value and an accepted fixture fails the suite naming itself, the
TC-017 expected failure carries a negative control that proves the binding is
real, and the reproducibility test compares against the committed bytes rather
than only against a second fresh run.
