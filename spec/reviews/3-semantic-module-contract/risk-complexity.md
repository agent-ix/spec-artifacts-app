# SR-007: Risk and complexity review of the #3 semantic-module contract spec set

## Summary

Technical-risk and volatility scoring of StR-001, US-001, FR-001..FR-005 and
NFR-001 on branch `spec/3-semantic-module-contract` at `be4e64b`, calibrated
against the two repositories that already shipped this exact migration:
`spec-objects-business#4` (merged `567e5c4`, 114 files, +9,891 lines, 17 emitted
schemas, a 10-task plan bundle) and `spec-artifacts-iso#34` (merged `6686f11`,
166 files, +16,325 lines, 10 models, 64 matrix rows, a 10-task plan bundle, an
871-line reference-mapping oracle). Their post-merge code reviews
(`spec-objects-business/reviews/26-09-03-semantic-data-schemas-code-review.md`,
SR-009; `spec-artifacts-iso/reviews/26-09-04-code-review-semantic-data-schemas-34.md`,
SR-012) are the empirical record of what this class of change actually gets
wrong, and they are the calibration source for the register below.

This lens does not re-litigate the contradictions the sibling reviews carry.
`base.md`, `integrity.md`, `scope-boundary.md`, `failure-domain.md`,
`dependency.md` and `ears-conformance.md` between them hold eleven high
findings; the dispositions in `b2f4cfa` and `be4e64b` closed several of them
(the `semantic.imports` shape is now package-to-exact-version with the type list
moved to `mappings.yaml`; `quoin#336`, `#338`, `#339`, `quire-rs#391`, `#393`
are now named in Out of Scope; the cycle graph is now built from synthesized
fixtures; `## Properties` now has a model property, a locator pair and an
`alternate_form` mapping entry; NFR-001 gained four addressable criteria). What
this lens adds is the cost of that motion and the shape of the remaining
exposure.

Three things dominate. **First**, the requirement set is still moving: two
disposition passes in roughly 48 hours rewrote 232 lines and deleted 79 across
the eight requirement documents, and every pass *added* obligations rather than
removing scope — the emitted file set went from unstated to a hand-enumerated
39, FR-004 grew an `alternate_form` and a defaulted `sourceSpan` rule, FR-003
grew `imported_types`, an over-declared-import diagnostic and a self-import
diagnostic. **Second**, the delivered comparators say this is an eight-to-ten
task change of ten to sixteen thousand lines, and the dependency review's own
topological order has nothing parallel in the feature layer after step 3.
**Third**, 4,599 lines of implementation across 55 files (including
`typespec/main.tsp` at 409 lines and all 39 emitted schemas) already sit on the
branch against a spec that four reviews call not ready, while `tests/` still
holds one file and `quire coverage` reports zero backed rows — so rework now
lands on generated bytes and digests, not on prose.

Sixteen findings: 3 high, 8 medium, 5 low. Scoring: FR-002 High risk / Medium
volatility; FR-003 High / High; FR-004 High / High; FR-005 Medium / High;
FR-001 Low / Low; NFR-001 Medium / Medium; StR-001 and US-001 Low / Low.

## Verdict

**Conditionally ready for `spec-to-plan`, and not ready for further
implementation.** No finding in this lens is a contradiction — the sibling
reviews own those — but FND-500, FND-501 and FND-502 together say the change is
being built at model-repo scale, on a single unparallelisable chain, ahead of a
spec whose obligation surface has grown at roughly a hundred lines per review
pass. The plan should be authored now precisely so the remaining spec motion
lands as task edits rather than as edits to 39 committed schemas and two
digests. FND-503..FND-510 are plan constraints and small spec edits that fit in
one authoring pass; the five lows are recorded for the plan's risk section.

### Top hazards

These are the five to review live before `spec-to-plan` runs.

1. **FND-502 / FND-501** — implementation is 4,599 lines ahead of a spec still
   under CHANGES REQUIRED, and the spec is still growing. Decide whether the
   remaining sibling highs land as spec edits before the plan or as plan tasks
   after it; either is workable, drifting between them is not.
2. **FND-500** — size and sequence. Eight to ten tasks on the iso shape, or one
   PR the size of `spec-artifacts-iso#34`. Choose before tasking.
3. **FND-507** — no green baseline: `make dev-quire` does not exist, the schema
   targets do not exist, and the policy is fail-not-skip. Task 0 or the whole
   build runs red.
4. **FND-503** — seven defects the two comparators already paid for, none of
   which any criterion here would catch. Cheapest risk reduction available.
5. **FND-508** — three unreleased upstream revisions and two open amendments
   under a manifest that binds exact bytes. Pin the FR-035 revision and
   sequence the `semantic` block last.

## Recommendations

1. **Plan shape (FND-500).** Ten tasks on the `spec-artifacts-iso#34` template:
   locators and skeleton structure; TypeSpec models; projection suite and drift
   gate; manifest `semantic` block and digests; record-shape gate; mappings and
   golden records; mapping-failure fixtures; negative fixtures; packaging
   conformance; reproducibility evidence. Order them as `dependency.md`'s
   topological sequence, and make packaging and reproducibility their own tasks
   rather than trailers on FR-002.
2. **Sequencing against the open highs (FND-501, FND-502).** Write the FR-002
   projection suite before any further schema emission, and hold the manifest
   `semantic` block bytes until the `quoin#336` posture and the FND-100
   declaration-record question are dispositioned. Both decisions cost prose
   today and 39 files plus two digests tomorrow.
3. **Carry the comparators' defects as constraints (FND-503).** Add the seven
   SR-009/SR-012 items to the plan's quality gates, and restate FR-002-AC-5's
   second clause as "a fresh `make schemas` reproduces the committed bundle
   byte-for-byte" so the reproducibility test compares against the tree of
   record rather than against itself.
4. **Coverage as an exit gate (FND-504).** `quire coverage` 76/76 in the plan's
   exit criteria; single-line `trace` markers; no stacked markers on one test.
5. **Bound the oracle (FND-505).** One fixture per FR-004 refusal rule, oracle
   line coverage measured and gated, and FR-004-AC-6's five refusals split off
   TC-025 so a red row names the rule that broke.
6. **One version-bump target (FND-506).** `manifest.yaml` `version`, the
   `@jsonSchema` base, the bundle and the digests change in one run and one
   commit; state the three-way agreement rule in FR-002 Behavior beside the
   existing one-directional guard in AC-9.
